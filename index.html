User Data Deletion Instructions

Skycare Compounding Labs (order.sclabs.ca) respects your privacy. If you logged in via Facebook or any social login and want your data deleted:

  1. Email privacy@sclabs.ca from the same email address used for your account.
  2. Use the subject line: Request for Account Deletion.
  3. Your account and related data will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.
  4. You will receive a confirmation email when deletion is complete.

You can also remove app permissions from your Facebook account at any time: Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites → Digits Social Login → Remove.

Questions? Contact privacy@sclabs.ca.
